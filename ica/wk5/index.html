The Flexbox and any layout tools for that matter constrain users' experiences to something closer to the creators vision. By allowing for dynamic elements at most device sizes the end user is more likely to experience the website as intended. By hiding information under different panels it can often be easier to find what you want because it likely is contained under a listed parent category. Those visiting the website with a screen reader might have a hard time processing the information on the website if everything is under nested subcategories.

By having users click to reveal certain parts of your website, you are underlining the importance of non hidden sections and recognizing that hidden sections are less likely to be the primary reason why a user visited the website. In terms of the community resource project anything that relates to urgent issues might be something that is mentioned clearly in the top bar. At any point in designing a website there is a balance between ease of access and information overload, a good designer is able to find that balance.
